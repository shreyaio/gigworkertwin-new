## Cover sheet

Start · Stop · 1 of 9

### Research survey — Your work with Uber

This survey asks how the Uber app manages your work and how changes to Uber's rules would
affect you. It takes about 36 minutes.

**Before you start**

- There are no right or wrong answers.
- You can skip any question or stop at any time.
- Your answers are anonymous. This research is not run by Uber.

**About this research**

This research studies how people who drive with Uber in India experience algorithmic
management, the software mediated decisions on trip matching, trip allocation, pay, and control
that the Uber app makes in place of human supervisors. The research has three objectives:

- To describe how Uber drivers perceive algorithmic control, algorithmic fairness, and
  transparency on Uber.
- To identify combinations of Uber redesign choices that would reduce driver resistance and
  turnover, and improve driver voice.
- To generate evidence that Uber, its product management teams, and regulators can use to
  redesign algorithmic management, prioritise app features, and design worker protection
  instruments.

Your participation contributes to the third objective. The survey takes approximately 36 minutes
to complete, including short questions answered in your own words, one short scenario, and six
short ratings of possible changes to Uber. Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question
or withdraw at any time without giving a reason. Your responses will be anonymised, aggregated
with those of other participants, and reported only in composite form. No individual worker will
be identifiable in any output from this research.

**Your rights**

You have the right to be informed about (a) the purpose of this research, (b) the identity of the
principal investigator, (c) how your data will be used, (d) how long your data will be stored, and
(e) how you can withdraw. All five points are set out on this sheet before you are asked to
consent.

Your responses will be anonymised at the point of data entry. No identifier (name, phone number,
platform username, vehicle number, or account number) will be linked to your responses in any
research output. Data will be stored on encrypted media accessible only to the principal
investigator and the appointed research team. Findings will be reported only in aggregate form.
No individual worker will be identifiable in any thesis, paper, presentation, or other output that
arises from this research.

Your data will be used only for academic research on algorithmic management of gig workers, as
set out in the three objectives in Section I. Data will not be sold, shared with your platform,
shared with any commercial third party, or used for marketing. Data will be retained in encrypted
form for five years from the date of collection. At the end of the five year period the primary
dataset will be destroyed, and only summary statistics will remain in the archived thesis.

You may withdraw at any time during the survey by closing the browser or by asking the
interviewer to stop. No reason is required, and no partial responses will be retained. After
completing the survey, you may withdraw your responses within 14 days by writing to the principal
investigator at the address below. To withdraw, quote your response reference number (issued at
the end of the survey) and state that you wish to withdraw.

After 14 days your responses will have been anonymised and merged into the aggregate dataset, at
which point they can no longer be identified for individual withdrawal. The aggregate data
continues to be used as described above.
